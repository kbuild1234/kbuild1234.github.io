General enquiries only

Do not send symptoms, diagnoses, medical records, employee health details, NHS numbers, passwords or other sensitive information. This form is not a clinical or emergency service and is not continuously monitored.

For a medical or confidential enquiry, call +44 7340 677 361 to arrange an appropriate secure follow-up. For a medical emergency, call 999.

Up to 4,000 characters. No attachments or medical information.

How your enquiry is handled

CocoHealth Ltd uses your name, reply email, company (if supplied) and message to handle and respond to your enquiry. Your submission passes through our contact server to our email provider and business inbox. It is not a secure medical-records service.

The relay does not save message bodies to disk or application logs. Enquiries remain in our email systems for handling and follow-up; this form does not automatically delete inbox copies. Temporary IP-based rate limits help prevent abuse. Call us about access, deletion or other privacy questions.

Using an AI assistant?

An agent acting with your permission can submit a general enquiry using our OpenAPI specification. No API key, browser session or CAPTCHA is required.

Agents must show you the message and obtain your approval before sending it. The same contact data notice, no-medical-information restriction and rate limits apply.

A success response means our email server accepted the enquiry for delivery, not that it has been read or reached the inbox. If delivery is unconfirmed, telephone us rather than automatically resending.
