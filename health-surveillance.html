Services

Build the clinic around the exposure risk

Health surveillance should be selected from the employer's risk assessment and applicable requirements. We can help translate the identified exposure groups into a practical clinic schedule.

Audiometry

Hearing surveillance for workers exposed to workplace noise where surveillance is indicated.

Spirometry

Respiratory health surveillance for relevant workplace exposures and risk groups.

HAVS

Tiered hand-arm vibration surveillance, with escalation pathways where further clinical assessment is required.

Skin surveillance

Skin assessment for roles with relevant exposure to irritants, sensitisers or frequent wet work.

Vision screening

Workplace vision screening that can be integrated into wider site clinic programmes.

Combined clinic

Multiple surveillance modalities can be coordinated for the same workforce or site day.
